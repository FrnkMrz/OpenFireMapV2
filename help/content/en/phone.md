## Overview {#ueberblick}

:::figure mobile-menu frame=phone set=mobile spots=menu,status,layers,share,search,legal,locate
OpenFireMap on a smartphone with the menu open.
:::

1. **Menu:** opens all tools.
2. **Status box:** zoom level and data date.
3. **Map layers:** change the background.
4. **Share Map View:** a link to this view.
5. **Search:** find a place or address.
6. **Info & Legal:** help, legal notice, privacy, licences.
7. **My location:** jump to where you are.

The menu also has **Map symbols** (every symbol explained) and the **local cache** (how long loaded areas are kept).

OpenFireMap switches to this view on devices with a touchscreen or when the browser window is narrower than 1370 pixels. That is why tablets usually show the menu too.

## Using your fingers {#gesten}

- **Pan:** drag with one finger.
- **Zoom:** pinch with two fingers, or double-tap.
- **Tap a hydrant:** from zoom level 16 a circle shows a 100 metre radius; after “My location”, from zoom level 17 also a line with the distance from you.
- **Close:** tap next to a menu.

## Search and location {#standort}

“Search” in the menu opens a search field above the map. After tapping “Go” the map jumps to the place.

The round button at the bottom centre shows your location and zooms to level 17. Your phone asks for permission the first time. Outdoors with GPS the position is usually accurate to a few metres.

On site, **Satellite** often helps with orientation, and at night **Night** is easier on the eyes. [Layers & symbols](../layers/) explains each background.

## Status box {#status}

The top right shows zoom level and data date. Tapping the box opens “Data status & origin” with the source, the age of the data and the live update. It also links to the status page and to “Info & Legal”.

## Start it like an app {#app}

OpenFireMap needs no installation. You can put the map on your home screen, though, and open it like an app without the browser bar.

:::steps
1. **iPhone and iPad (Safari):** open openfiremap.org, tap **Share** at the bottom, then choose **Add to Home Screen**.
2. **Android (Chrome):** open openfiremap.org, tap **⋮** at the top right, then choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
:::

:::note You still need internet as an app
New areas, search and current data come from the network. Areas you have loaded stay in memory for a while, depending on the cache setting. On the road without a connection, a printed [town plan](../town-plan/) is more reliable.
:::

## Only on a computer {#nur-computer}

**Export** (PNG, PDF, GPX, CSV) and the **town plan** need a large screen for selecting the area and a lot of memory. Both are therefore only available on a computer. Open openfiremap.org on a PC or laptop. The share link lets you take your view from the phone along.
